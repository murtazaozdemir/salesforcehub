Xhibit

X posts, preserved with proof anyone can check. Same-day capture of posts and X Spaces for law firms, sized for a handful of posts, not terabytes.

  • Each capture gets a SHA-256 fingerprint and a Bitcoin timestamp through OpenTimestamps
  • Every step is written to a tamper-evident custody log
  • Checks against X's public embed service, and flags posts that are later deleted
  • X Spaces: who took part, the chat and the recording
  • A declaration with exhibits, exported to Word or PDF

Offered as a done-for-you service, at a flat fee per matter quoted up front.

Ask about a matter

Salesforce Hub LLC is not a law firm and doesn't give legal advice. Whether evidence is admissible is for the court to decide. Xhibit shows what an account posted, not who typed it, and captures only what a standard signed-in X account can see. Not affiliated with X Corp.
